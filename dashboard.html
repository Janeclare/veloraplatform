Quick Answers

How much does Nextel pay per subscriber?

Nextel pays a base rate of ₦395.5 per subscriber per month across all 6 NetGain streams. You also earn ₦50/min on NexCall, ₦12,000 per NexSim activation, referral bonuses, and milestone rewards.

How long does verification take?

Manual verification takes 1–24 hours after payment. We will confirm your account once the transfer is processed.

What is the Network Referral Bonus?

The Network Referral Bonus is ₦15,000 credited to your account for every subscriber you successfully recruit to Nextel. You also receive a ₦10,000 Network Milestone Reward on top, plus ₦400 for 1st indirect commissions.

How fast are withdrawals processed?

Nextel processes withdrawals within 24 hours to local Nigerian bank accounts. Contact our Telegram admin at t.me for withdrawal requests.
